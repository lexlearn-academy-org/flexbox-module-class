My Process

Phases

  • 01 Discovery
  • 02 Define
  • 03 Design
  • 04 Test
  • 05 Deliver

Discovery

I start every project by understanding the people who will use the product. This means stakeholder interviews, user interviews, competitive analysis, and reviewing any existing research. I come out of discovery with a clear picture of user goals, pain points, and the business constraints I am designing within.

Define

I synthesise discovery findings into user personas, journey maps, and a clear problem statement. This stage produces the brief that guides all design decisions. Getting this right prevents scope creep and keeps teams aligned throughout the project.

Design

I move from low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes in Figma. I design for the edge cases first, not last. Every interaction is documented — not just the happy path.

Test

I run moderated usability sessions with five to eight participants per round. Sessions are recorded, transcribed, and turned into a prioritised list of improvements before anything is handed to engineering.

Deliver

I hand off to developers using annotated Figma files, a component inventory, and a live design system where applicable. I stay involved through the build phase to answer questions and review implementation quality.
